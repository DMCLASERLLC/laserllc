DMC Design Services LLC

A design fabrication studio built around making things work.

40+ years designing, engineering, and producing displays for retail mass merchandising, luxury cosmetics, and liquor.

We combine detailed 3D design and engineering with practical shop capabilities to take projects from concept through prototype and production.

Our work includes SolidWorks 3D assemblies for injection-molded parts, fabricated plastic parts, vacuum-formed parts, sheet metal and steel wire components, retail fixtures, corrugated and board displays, signs, and laser-cut lettering.

We build prototypes and handle short-run production of wood and plastic displays. We also create instruction sheets for assembling complex corrugated liquor displays, along with planograms and retail retrofits.

Design knowledge backed by hands-on production.

From the first 3D assembly to a finished prototype or short production run, DMC Design Services can help move your project forward.
